Support learning at home and in class

Quizphoria offers short, focused quizzes by grade. This is what each grade covers.

Grade 1

Counting and adding small numbers, letter sounds, greetings in Kiswahili, pets, water and the sun.

Grade 2

Adding and taking away, short sentences and opposites, days of the week, plants and animals.

Grade 3

Multiplying and halves, past tense and plurals, everyday Kiswahili words, water sources and soil care.

Grade 4

Times tables, fractions and perimeter, adjectives and synonyms, Kiswahili grammar and methali, plants and the senses.

Grade 5

Area, percentages and prime numbers, adverbs and similes, wakati uliopita, energy, the water cycle and the body.
